PROFESSIONAL PROFILE

About Me

I am an Electrical Design Engineer with experience delivering coordinated electrical building-services designs for commercial, residential, healthcare, airport, infrastructure and other multidisciplinary developments.

My technical experience includes lighting and emergency lighting, LV power distribution, load assessment, cable sizing, voltage-drop calculations, containment design, earthing, lightning protection, fire alarm, UPS and standby-power systems.

I prepare electrical layouts, schematics, technical calculations, equipment schedules, BIM models, coordinated drawings, IFC documentation and construction deliverables.

I work with Revit, AutoCAD, Navisworks, DIALux evo, ElectricalOM and other specialist electrical-design software. My experience includes projects based on BS 7671, CIBSE guidance, IEC standards and applicable regional requirements.

I am also developing my knowledge of AS/NZS 3000 and Australian and New Zealand building-services practices to support future international opportunities.
